# Mind Compass — Decision Register v1.0

## Approved direction

1. Mind Compass is a distinct experience within mindRID, not a replacement for the Social World.
2. Development begins embryonically now while preserving the approved V19–V21 sequence.
3. The visual references inform a cosmic, luminous map, a four-direction compass and constellations.
4. A Whisper may be represented as a meteor-like journey; the original Whisper remains canonical.
5. The central conceptual lens is that expression may have a protective function: perceived threat → vulnerability → defensive response → intended protection, with possible costs and alternatives.
6. Mind Compass may gradually coach users to notice attention, understand patterns, discern, choose, practise and reorient.
7. For users who opt in, a long-term purpose may include seeking Allah's satisfaction; the product must not claim divine judgment or coerce faith.
8. V18.5 remains the known production baseline unless a later release is verified.

## Non-negotiable constraints

- No drift without structured discussion and explicit founder approval.
- Privacy, consent, uncertainty, user agency, deletion and accessibility are architectural requirements.
- No covert dopamine/reward exploitation, addiction loops, spiritual scoring or guilt-based engagement.
- No diagnoses or assertions of unconscious motive as fact.
- No Compass migration or production tracking before schema/security inventory and staging validation.

## Proposed, not yet approved implementation details

- Exact Compass entry point in the signed-in UI.
- Exact database table/column names and migration structure.
- Whether AI interpretation ships in the first pilot or follows a user-authored-only pilot.
- Exact visual effects, animation, rendering library and device-performance targets.
- Shared constellations membership and moderation details.

## Open gates

- Complete V19.0 repository and live-schema/security inventory.
- Confirm whether the actual codebase still matches the V18.5 baseline.
- Verify current Supabase RLS, grants, storage rules, auth and deletion/export behaviour.
- Founder review of the foundation package before implementation begins.

## Change control

Any proposal that changes the destination, spiritual orientation, privacy defaults, role of emotional-defence interpretation, or approved roadmap order must be presented as a structured decision with rationale, impact, alternatives and risks. Research may trigger review but does not silently reverse an approved decision.

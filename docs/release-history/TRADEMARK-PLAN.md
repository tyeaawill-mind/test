# mindRID Brand Protection — Action Plan

This is an implementation/business checklist, not legal advice.

## Immediate protection targets
1. Word mark: **mindRID**
2. Stylized/logo mark used by mindRID
3. Consider protection for any distinctive product name that becomes a permanent feature name, after a clearance search.
4. Preserve evidence of first commercial/public use: domain registration, dated product builds, screenshots, launch materials and business records.

## Bangladesh
The Department of Patents, Designs and Trademarks (DPDT) is the relevant government office. DPDT's current online-application information lists TM-1 for a trademark registration application and TM-4 for a search application. Use the current DPDT portal/forms and confirm the appropriate class(es), applicant ownership and filing requirements with a Bangladesh trademark professional before filing.

## International expansion
If protection outside Bangladesh becomes important, assess national filings and the Madrid System with counsel. WIPO states that the Madrid System can provide international registration covering designated Contracting Parties, subject to the applicable requirements.

## Before filing
- Run a clearance search for “mindRID” and confusingly similar marks.
- Search relevant goods/services classes, not just the exact spelling.
- Confirm ownership: individual founder vs company/entity.
- Confirm the exact logo/stylization to be filed.
- Decide the jurisdictions based on actual commercial plans.
- Have counsel check domain/social/marketplace conflicts separately from trademark registry results.
